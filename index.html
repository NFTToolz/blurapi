Common questions

Useful details before you call.

Where is AV Support based?

AV Support is based in Williams Landing, Victoria 3027. Contact Andrew to discuss your suburb and whether travel is practical.

Who will provide the support?

Andrew is a self-employed support worker and the person behind AV Support. The site is built around direct one-to-one support rather than presenting AV Support as a large agency.

What kinds of support can I ask about?

The main areas are respite and overnight support, community outings and activities, and gaming or shared-interest sessions. If you need something related, ask Andrew whether it falls within his experience and availability.

Can I use NDIS funding?

Funding arrangements depend on your plan and individual circumstances. Contact Andrew before booking so the funding and service arrangement can be confirmed.

How do I check current availability?

Call or text 0435 740 880. Because this is independent support, availability can change and is best confirmed directly.
