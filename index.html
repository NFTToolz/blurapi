About Andrew

Qualified support without the agency feel.

Andrew is a self-employed support worker and the person behind AV Support. He provides one-to-one support tailored to the individual, with a focus on safety, respect, inclusion and building a genuine long-term connection.

He holds a Certificate IV in Disability and a Certificate II in Aged Care, and brings eight years of continuous experience supporting the same participant.

Common questions

Useful details before you call.

Who provides the support?

Andrew is the support worker behind AV Support and is the person you will speak with directly. The website is built around direct one-to-one support rather than presenting AV Support as a large provider.

What support can I ask about?

The main areas are respite and overnight support, community outings and activities, and gaming or shared-interest sessions. If you need something related, ask Andrew whether it suits his experience and availability.

Where is AV Support based?

AV Support is based in Williams Landing, Victoria 3027, and primarily services Melbourne's western suburbs.

Can I use NDIS funding?

Funding arrangements depend on your plan and circumstances. It is best to contact Andrew directly so the service arrangement can be discussed before anything is booked in.

How do I check availability?

The easiest way is to call or text 0435 740 880. Let Andrew know your suburb, the kind of support you're looking for and your preferred days or times.
